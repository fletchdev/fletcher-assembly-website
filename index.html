02 About

Games with a
sense of place.

Fletcher is an independent game developer focused on atmospheric games, environmental storytelling and distinctive worlds.

Fletcher Assembly is where those ideas come together: slow-building tension, spaces that feel lived in and then abandoned, and details players discover for themselves rather than being told.

Collaborators join the work where a project calls for them. The direction stays focused and personal.
